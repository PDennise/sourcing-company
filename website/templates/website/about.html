About Us

Your Team on the Ground in Turkey

Turkey offers exceptional manufacturing capabilities across a wide range of industries.

But successful sourcing requires more than finding a supplier.

It requires knowledge, clear communication, continuity, and a trusted partner who understands both sides of the relationship.

At Pandarex, we help international buyers navigate the Turkish supplier market with confidence. We act as an extension of our clients’ teams, providing local expertise, supplier insight, and hands-on support throughout the sourcing process.

Why Work With Us

After years of working in export sales and international business, we repeatedly saw the same challenge: international buyers were not struggling to find products. They were struggling to find reliable local support.

Many companies wanted to source from Turkey but lacked a trusted partner on the ground — someone who understood the market, could communicate effectively with suppliers, protect their interests, and help them make informed decisions.

We also saw how easily sourcing projects could become more complicated than necessary. Contacts changed, important information was lost, communication became inconsistent, response times slowed, and opportunities were missed.

The problem was rarely the product itself. More often, the challenge was communication, continuity, transparency, and trust.

That is why Pandarex was created.
